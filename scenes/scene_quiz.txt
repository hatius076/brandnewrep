# Quiz Scene Template

This scene tests the AI's memory of previously collected facts.

## Flow:
1. AI announces memory test
2. 4 sequential recall questions:
   - What is your name?
   - What is your favorite food?
   - What is your hobby?
   - Where do you relax?

## Memory Accuracy:
- Character A: 100% accuracy (perfect recall)
- Character B: ~50% accuracy (controlled errors, max 3)

## Question Format:
- Multiple choice with 3-4 options
- Correct answer + plausible distractors
- AI selects answer automatically after brief delay

## Error Handling:
- Character B may select wrong answers
- Optional apologetic responses for errors
- Maintains natural conversation flow
