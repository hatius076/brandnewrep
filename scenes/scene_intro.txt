# Introduction Scene Template

This scene handles the initial greeting and fact collection phase.

## Flow:
1. AI greets the player
2. Collects 6 personal facts:
   - Name
   - Favorite food
   - Favorite hobby
   - Relaxation place
   - Profession
   - Bonus fact

## Memory Implementation:
- Character A: Perfect retention of all facts
- Character B: May experience memory lapses during quiz phase

## Response Generation:
- Responses are generated dynamically based on player input
- Response length limited to ≤20 tokens
- Consistent tone and helpfulness across both characters
